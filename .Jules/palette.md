## 2024-06-19 - Accessible Framer Motion Menus\n**Learning:** Custom animated dropdowns in Framer Motion often lack native keyboard and screen reader accessibility by default, which hides functionality from assistive technologies.\n**Action:** Always implement `role="menu"`, `role="menuitem"`, `aria-haspopup`, and `aria-expanded` attributes, alongside Escape key and click-outside dismissal handlers, to ensure custom menus meet WCAG standards while maintaining smooth animations.
