## 2023-10-27 - Custom Animated Dropdowns Require Explicit ARIA Roles and Keyboard Management
**Learning:** Custom components like animated dropdowns (e.g., using Framer Motion) inherently lack the semantic structure and keyboard interactions of native `<select>` or `<menu>` elements. Without explicitly declaring `aria-haspopup`, `aria-expanded`, and mapping elements to `role="menu"` and `role="menuitem"`, screen readers cannot interpret the UI's purpose. Furthermore, users expecting native behavior rely on the Escape key and click-outside interactions to safely dismiss menus without making a selection.
**Action:** When building custom interactive components, always supplement them with matching ARIA roles and ensure focus management (e.g., returning focus to the trigger on Escape) is explicitly wired up.
